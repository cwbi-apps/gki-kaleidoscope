Geospatial Knowledge Portal

This site hosts two connected tools. One helps you ask questions of geospatial knowledge using AI; the other helps you build and manage the authoritative geospatial data behind it. Pick the one that fits what you're trying to do.

Explore & ask

Kaleidoscope

A Geo-AI explorer for chatting with the geospatial knowledge graph in plain language.

  • Ask questions about places, features, and how they relate
  • See answers on a map alongside the conversation
  • Discover data without knowing the underlying schema
Open Kaleidoscope

Build & manage

GeoPrism Registry

A data builder for creating, curating, and publishing authoritative geospatial master data.

  • Define geo-object types and hierarchies
  • Import, edit, and validate geographic data over time
  • Publish versioned lists and share data with other systems

Not sure where to start? If you want to find answers or explore what's known about a place, use Kaleidoscope. If you need to add, change, or maintain the data itself, use GeoPrism Registry.
